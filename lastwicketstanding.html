How It Works

The basics

1

Each round, pick one of the six ILT20 teams that you think will win their match.

2

Your team loses — you're out for the season. Your team wins — you go through to the next round.

3

Every team must be picked once before any team can be picked a second time. No team can ever be used more than twice across the whole season.

4

Survive the 10-round league stage, then the playoffs, and you're the last one standing.

Why the "pick every team once" rule?

With only six teams in the ILT20, it would be easy to just pick the strongest side every single round. The rule forces everyone through all six teams once before any repeats — so no single "safe" pick can carry you through the whole season. It's what keeps a real prize genuinely hard to win, not just a formality.

The league stage — Rounds 1 to 10

Every team plays every other team twice across 10 rounds (a double round-robin), with 3 matches happening each round. Your first 6 picks must be 6 different teams. From Round 7 onward, you can start repeating a team you've already used once — but never a third time.

The playoffs — Rounds 11 to 14

The top 4 teams from the league stage go through to a knockout playoff bracket. Anyone still alive after the league stage keeps picking, one team per match, right through to the Final:

Qualifier 11st place v 2nd place. Winner goes straight to the Final. Loser gets a second chance.
Eliminator3rd place v 4th place. Loser is knocked out of the tournament entirely.
Qualifier 2Loser of Qualifier 1 v winner of the Eliminator. Winner goes to the Final.
FinalWinner of Qualifier 1 v winner of Qualifier 2. Winner takes the title.

Picks in the playoffs are fresh — no restriction on repeating a team here, since a team can legitimately appear in more than one playoff match.

Winning

The last player (or players, if more than one survives to the same point) still standing wins. If everyone remaining is knocked out in the same round — a clean sweep with nobody left — there's no winner that season, and the whole league rolls straight over into a fresh season with everyone back in.

Free to play

Entry is completely free. Where a prize is offered, it's provided by the league's sponsor — never funded by anyone's entry. See the Terms & Conditions for details.
